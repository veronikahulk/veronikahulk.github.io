Privacy Policy

Factland Uploader · last updated 18 November 2026

Factland Uploader is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Factland YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not available to the public.

What data the tool accesses

  • The Factland channel's own videos, their metadata and statistics (YouTube Data API).
  • Permission to upload videos, schedule them and post comments as the Factland channel.

The tool does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or any other people, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

How access is stored

The access token is stored only in the channel owner's private, encrypted environment settings and is used solely to manage the Factland channel.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
